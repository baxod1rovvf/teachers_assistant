# Listening Journey --- Steps 8--20

**Level:** A1+--A2\
**Includes:** Audio transcripts, listening questions, spelling practice,
and an answer key.

**Spelling convention:** Names of people, places, pets, streets,
schools, shops and other named locations are spoken and spelled aloud in
the recordings. Numbers are spoken clearly so students can practise
writing dates, times, prices, addresses, room numbers and phone numbers.

# Step 11 --- An Unforgettable Holiday

**Target:** 6 minutes \| **Format:** Personal story \| **Questions:** 16

## Audio transcript

Hello! My name is Amelia Brown. Amelia is A-M-E-L-I-A, and Brown is
B-R-O-W-N. Today I'll tell you about a holiday I remember very well.

Last summer, my family travelled to a small town near the mountains
called Silver Lake. Silver is S-I-L-V-E-R, and Lake is L-A-K-E. We went
by train. We left home on the tenth of July at six forty-five in the
morning. The journey took four hours, and we arrived at ten forty-five.

My uncle David was waiting at the station. David is D-A-V-I-D. He had
booked a guesthouse called Mountain View. It had five rooms, a small
garden and a restaurant. Our room had two beds and a balcony with a view
of the mountains.

On the first day, we walked around the town. In the evening, we ate at a
local restaurant. I ordered vegetable soup and grilled chicken. My
younger sister Sophie chose pasta. Sophie is S-O-P-H-I-E.

The next morning, we went hiking with a guide called Nora. Nora is
N-O-R-A. She showed us a safe walking path. We saw birds and a small
fox. Later, we stopped near a waterfall and took photographs. The
weather was sunny at first, but it became cloudy and windy.

On the third day, we visited a farm. There were goats, sheep, chickens
and two friendly dogs. One dog was called Max. Max is M-A-X. He followed
us around and played with my sister.

We stayed for five days and returned home on the fifteenth of July. I
loved the mountain walk, but Sophie said playing with Max was the best
part. It was a wonderful holiday because we spent time together and
discovered a new place.

# Step 17 --- Healthy Habits and Daily Life

**Target:** 8 minutes \| **Format:** Lifestyle podcast \| **Questions:**
19

## Audio transcript

Hello, and welcome to Everyday Choices, a podcast about simple habits
that can make daily life better. I'm your presenter, Rachel Moore.
Rachel is R-A-C-H-E-L, and Moore is M-O-O-R-E.

Today we're talking about healthy habits. Some people think a healthy
lifestyle requires expensive food, special equipment or lots of free
time. However, many useful habits are simple.

Let's begin with sleep. Having a regular sleeping routine can help you
feel more organised. If you wake up at seven, you might choose to go to
bed at around ten thirty or eleven. People need different amounts of
sleep, but enough rest is important.

The second habit is drinking water. Some people forget to drink during
busy days. One useful idea is to keep a bottle nearby. My bottle holds
seven hundred and fifty millilitres. I fill it in the morning and take
it to work.

Another habit is eating a variety of foods. Fruit, vegetables, grains
and other foods can all be part of balanced meals. You don't need to eat
the same thing every day. You could have eggs and bread for breakfast,
rice with vegetables for lunch, and soup for dinner.

music and spending time with friends can help people relax.

## Recording and assessment notes

-   Pause briefly before and after spelling names: "Layla --- L... A...
    Y... L... A."
-   Read phone numbers in small groups, then repeat them once naturally.
-   Say dates clearly, for example, "the fifteenth of May."
-   Read prices with both dollars and cents.
-   Do not show students the transcript before the listening activity.
-   For spelling questions, accept correct uppercase or lowercase
    letters.
-   For number questions, decide whether students should write digits or
    words; accept either if the answer is unambiguous.
