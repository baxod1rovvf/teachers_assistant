# How the AI check works (after Submit)

1. **Final score out of 9** — Grammar 3 · Vocabulary 2 · Sentence structure & coherence 2 · Task completion 2; then −0.5 if the writing is shorter than the minimum (never for writing more).
2. **Word count** — the student's words and the required words (counted by Loom, exactly).
3. **Grammar mistakes** — the wrong sentence, the corrected sentence and a simple explanation.
4. **Better words** — optional suggestions (e.g. very good → excellent), not counted as mistakes.
5. **Asked words checklist** — used ✅ / missing ❌ (other forms count: use → used).
6. **Three short tips** for the next writing.
