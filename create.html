🥫 Can Knockdown

Tin cans on a table in a real-looking room. Students see an English word on a paper (e.g. "hello") and type its translation (e.g. "salom"). Every right answer throws a ball and knocks down a can. 6 cans make a round — they reach the next round only when all 6 words are found (a missed word comes back). A translation written like "salom / assalomu alaykum" accepts either.

🌴 Jungle

A board game for the classroom screen. The class splits into 2, 3 or 4 teams, each with its own token. A team rolls the dice and moves that many squares: rolling 6 means question 6, then rolling 3 next time means question 9. Every square is one of your questions, and a question can have a picture under it.

🎲 6 🦁 Question 6 🐒🌴

Teams (2, 3 or 4) are chosen when the game starts. In the game, a team's token can also be dragged to any square, and clicking a square shows its question.

🎯 Bamboozle

A team quiz for the classroom screen, played with you (no student IDs). The class splits into 2–4 teams: red, blue, green and yellow. Numbered cards hide your questions and their points. A team picks a card, answers, and you press Correct or Incorrect. Then it's the next team's turn and their colour spreads across the screen. When every card is used, the winner is shown.

Red 25 Blue 30 🎯🏆

Each question can have its own points, an answer that only you reveal (👁 Show answer), and a picture. Team names can be changed when the game starts. About 40 cards fit on one screen.
