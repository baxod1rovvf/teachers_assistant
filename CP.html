How teachers get started

  1. Send them the same Teacher's Assistant file you use.
  2. Tell them their login and password from this page.
  3. They open the file and sign in. Everything they create stays in their own account.

Turning an account off, deleting it, or changing its password signs that teacher out the next time they open the app with internet.
